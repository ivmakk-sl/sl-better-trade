# Images

Nexus page images, tracked in the repo. The background is 1300x372, the gallery images are 1920x1080. The mods-grid thumbnail is not a separate file: Nexus picks it from the uploaded gallery images. The text is Noto Sans (Bold for the name and the titles, Light for the subtitles and the labels), white plus the gold `(251, 178, 11)`. The images were prepared by hand from 4K in-game screenshots on game 1.1.18293, with the window panels cut out onto black.

- `sl-better-trade-banner.jpg` - the mod page background image (1300x372): the trade window of the Safe House (the freezer grid with the cell numbers, the drone cargo, and the Offered list with the prices), with the trade bar (offer 220, price 308) laid over it at a tilt and arrows to its two numbers.
- `sl-better-trade-thumb.jpg` - title card (1920x1080): "Better Trade" with a coin icon, beside the Offered list with the prices and the Delivery Targets list with the reward strips, and the drone action menu with arrows from Trade Run to the Offered list and from Deliver Request to the Delivery Targets list. Pick this as the mods-grid thumbnail.
- `sl-better-trade-trade.jpg` - a trade (1920x1080): "Trading aka Trade Run", beside the trade window of the Safe House, with the labels "Underpriced (unwanted type)" at the header factor `Medicine x1/2`, "Prices" at the Offered list, and "Offer vs Price" at the bar numbers 220 and 308.
- `sl-better-trade-requests.jpg` - Deliver Request (1920x1080): "Delivery Request", beside the request window of the Harbor Dock Crew, with the labels "Quickly find what to deliver" at the meat dishes with their satiety numbers in the freezer grid, "Cleaner Delivery Panel", "Preview Rewards" at a reward strip, and "Track what is needed" at the demand rows of a request.
